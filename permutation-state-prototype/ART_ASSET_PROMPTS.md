**Asset path:** `outputs/permutation-state-prototype/assets/aster-map-v2.png`  
**Generation method:** built-in ImageGen  
**Built-in ImageGen mode:** `stylized-concept`

The following is the exact generation prompt:

```text
Primary request: a bright, readable aerial-isometric map of Aster, one shared river civilization, shown as a living civic system rather than a conquered territory; a luminous capital, several interconnected districts and worksites, an eastern river guild enclave, a visibly damaged sluice and dry terraces, roads and aqueducts forming a clear network
Scene/backdrop: one continuous fictional world region viewed from high above; playable terrain fills the frame; organic hex-like land parcels may be subtly implied but must not look like a board-game grid
Style/medium: “living atlas” aesthetic, hand-painted architectural cartography fused with bio-mineral scientific illustration; elegant, clean, tactile, optimistic speculative civilization; not medieval parchment and not glossy 3D
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide strategy-game map; river runs diagonally from cool fertile northwest through the center toward dry coral-toned east; clear named-location silhouettes without any written labels; enough quiet terrain near the left and right edges for translucent UI drawers; fine details remain readable under interface overlays
Lighting/mood: clear early morning after rain, luminous and alert rather than grim; the eastern crisis is visible through dry color and fractured water lines, not fire or apocalypse
Color palette: warm bone and chalk, pale mineral cyan, cobalt water, coral vermilion crisis accents, moss green, graphite shadows; high readability and restrained saturation
Materials/textures: ceramic relief, vellum fibers, translucent mineral veins, fine contour lines
Constraints: no text, no words, no numbers, no logos, no UI panels, no characters in close-up, no flags, no recognizable real-world culture, no copyrighted style imitation, no watermark
Avoid: brown medieval parchment, dark fantasy, castle-centric composition, photorealism, neon cyberpunk, generic mobile-game gloss, large empty sky, black background, visible interface
```
