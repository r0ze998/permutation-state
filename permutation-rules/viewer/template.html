What this match shows

Captured cities restart at half defence. With defence reset to 0, the same city changed hands after 1, 1 and 3 ticks. At 50%, the quickest recapture took two ticks of real fighting, and recaptures within 10 ticks fell from 5 to 2.

Peace now starts a 12-tick truce. Before, war was re-declared within 12 ticks of peace 6 times, riding on leftover grievances. Now war can only resume once the truce ends; the Warlord bot does exactly that at ticks 115 and 140.

Science races land in the Crisis window. Dunmar and Borealis finish all three Star Gate stages late in the match, inside the Crisis and Resolution phases, as the spec intends.

Bots are simple rule-based players reading the full state, so this is a rules test, not a showcase of play. The Crisis, markets and fog of war are not implemented yet.
