## 1. What landed

### Keeper duties (lib `keeper_core`)

| Module | Role(s) | Class | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rings` | `rings` | D | **Every ring by one code path** (the ring part of W2-F's `genesis` moved here; `genesis` keeps the genesis seed). `OpenRing(d)` for `d = rings_opened`: a genesis ring (`d ≤ g`) as soon as the season is Seeded; a crowding ring (`d > g`) only when `fclient::land::ring_open_check` holds on the **folded** values (Running, ≥ 1 bell since `last_ring_open_bell`, some wedge with `wedge_occupied × 10⁴ ≥ θ × wedge_open` where θ is early/late by `theta_switch_secs`, every wedge fund `≥ rent(128) + d × rent(4,096)`); the reason it waits is kept (`waiting`, in `/v1/status`). `ConsumeRingSeed(d)` once the RingSeed's round (`ring_seed_round(t_open)`) is published. `OpenProvince` for every missing province of every seeded ring; a ring is `complete` once all its 6d provinces are on chain. |
| `fold` | `fold` | D | FoldOccupancy every bell while `bell < join_close_bell` (Running): parts 0, 1, 2 **in order in the same bell** (the next part only after the previous landed; `next_part` reads the Frontier's `fold_bell`/`fold_part`, and remembers a landed part 2 for the bell-0 ambiguity of `fold_part = 0`). A part still pending when the bell turns is cancelled (it would be `FoldStale`). |
| `tickets` | `tickets` | D | SettleTicket for every open ticket once `S(ticket_bell, r_site)` exists. Candidates are grouped by their **current** site (`ticket_sites[ticket_next]`) and ordered by the lottery (score desc, then the lower `citizen_tag`); the site's mirror and Holding predict the outcome (`fclient::land::predict_site`): **fresh** → only the best ticket is in flight for that site, the next goes when it lands (one per site per slot); **taken** (held final, another cohort, a better score) → the whole group at once; **displace** (a provisional holding of the same cohort with a losing score, i.e. someone settled out of order) → the best ticket with the displaced holder's rent payer, Citizen and JoinShard. **Expired** (`now_bell ≥ ticket_bell + 24`) → settled `expired` at once. Each write is keyed `ticket:<citizen>:<ticket_bell>:<k>`, so a landed `taken` moves the next plan to preference `k + 1`. |
| `explore` | `explore` | N | SettleExplore for every Holding an `EXPLORE` record named whose explore record is pending (state 1), once `S(record.bell, region(record P, Q))` exists. |
| `holdings` | `dormancy`, `sweep` | N | Every `land_scan_slots` (default 8) the Holdings the feed named are read: `pool_owed > 0` → SweepPoolOwed (also at once for a Holding a `TRANSIT_SETTLED` record says owes the pool); order 1, `now ≥ last_owner_action + release_after`, no transit in state 1–3 → ReleaseDormant (to the Holding's rent payer). A Holding that disappears (RELEASE, or read absent) triggers a scan of every opened Province: an entry whose host id names a Holding that is absent or of another `gen` → DisbandStranded(entry). A backstop scan runs every `stranded_scan_slots` (default 450, 6 bells at 20×). |
| `landindex` | (all land roles) | — | Discovery from the program's feed (`ChainPort::feed`, successful transactions only, PS2 records via `frontier-abi::log`): JOIN (citizen → wallet, faction), TICKET (open tickets), SETTLE fresh/displace (holdings), EXPLORE (holdings with a record), RELEASE (stranded scan), TRANSIT_SETTLED with a `pool_owed` delta (sweep at once), PROVINCE_OPEN (provinces). The chain stays the state: every duty reads the accounts before it plans. A restarted keeper re-reads the feed from cursor 0. |
| `seeds` | — | — | Where `S(b, r)` is read and its value: the beacon duty's own cache (then the SeedCache account for the seed and its landing slot), else THE anchor + archive (an archived bell → `SeedSource::Archive` and the entry's seed), else one probe of the 256 nonces per 16 slots for a cache another keeper created. A cache seen before its bell was archived switches to the archive source. |
| `reveal_accept` | API | — | The `/v1/reveal` **accept path** (queue only; the pipeline is W4-C's): reads the Season, the Holding, THE anchor of `(arrive, region(dest))`, its half-day archive, the region's BeaconLog, ClashInputs `(dest, arrive)` and the destination Province at their canonical addresses and judges in the program's Reveal order: season takes reveals (Running, or Ended with `arrive < end_bell`) else `410`; transit slot in state 1–3 else `409 TransitState` (also for a missing Holding); `Plain::validate` against the transit's host and arrival bell else `422 BadPlaintext`; `sha256(commit(plain, salt) ‖ ct_hash) == seal_root` else `409 CommitMismatch`; THE anchor present → `now < A + W(arrive)` and `latest_round < S(A)` else `410 WindowClosed`; anchor absent → the archive must not tombstone `arrive` else `410`; ClashInputs present or `dest.resolved_next > arrive` → `410 WindowClosed` (detail `LatchClosed`). An anchor not yet posted is **not** a refusal (owners submit from the arrival bell's start). Accepted material is queued in `Shared.reveals` with `checked, host_id, faction, arrive, dest, tile, region, commit_hex, close`; the same `(holding, slot, commit)` answers the same track. |

`api` gained `router_with`/`serve_with(…, gate)`; `main` serves the API with a `ChainGate` over its own `RpcPort`. `config` gained `land_scan_slots` and `stranded_scan_slots`. `Keeper::tick` pulls the feed index and plans fold → tickets → explore → holdings after the beacon and archive duties; outcomes are routed to the fold and ticket duties **before** the duties plan; a land write that dies with `NotImplemented` (99) turns its role off with one `not-implemented` alert (the W2 program has no land); the expected refusals of a re-planned land write (`FoldStale` 44, `TicketState` 45, besides `NoAnchor`/`SeedNotReady`) are not alerted. `/v1/status` shows the land state (`provinces_opened`, `rings_complete`, `ring_waiting`, `folded_bells`, `tickets_open/settled/expired`, `ticket_winner_latency_slots_p99`, `explores_sent`, `releases_sent`, `sweeps_sent`, `disbands_sent`, `land_unsupported`).

## 4. Deviations and choices

| # | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | `ticket_score = rng::rand(S, "site", le_i32(P) ‖ le_i32(Q) ‖ site ‖ le_u64(citizen_tag))` | §5.9 names the fields, not their widths. These are the §4.1 raw widths (SETTLE's key, `explore::roll`'s id). The keeper uses the score only to **order** its burst and to **predict** a displacement; a different encoding in W3-A would make it settle in a non-lottery order (the program still decides, and a wrong displacement prediction is refused and re-planned), so it must be pinned (request K1). |
| D2 | Discovery from the program's feed, re-read from cursor 0 at start | `ChainPort` has no program-account scan; the feed is a chain read (the local node's `frontier_feed`; over a public RPC it is findex's RpcPoll, W2-F). The index is small and rebuilt, never trusted over the chain. Persisting the cursor in the journal is left for W5 hardening. |
| D3 | An expired ticket's settlement names the canonical cache address of nonce 0 when no seed of its bell is known | §5.9 decides `expired` before reading the seed; the positions must still be filled. If W3-A reads the seed accounts first, the keeper needs the real ones (request K2). |
| D4 | Fresh settlements of one site are serialized (the best first; the rest after it lands) instead of all sent in one slot | Transactions of one slot land in priority order, not send order, so sending the whole cohort at once cannot guarantee "descending score". One per site per slot is the burst: contested sites cost one slot per contender, different sites go in parallel (measured p99 3 slots). |
| D5 | Crowding rings may open once per bell until the fold counts the new ring's sites | That is the §5.9 rule as written (the fold sees a new ring's sites only after ConsumeRingSeed at `ring_seed_round(t_open) ≈ t_open + 660 s` and its OpenProvinces, i.e. ≥ 1 bell later). In the test θ = 10% opened rings 4, 5, 6 for one crowded wedge. Worth a contract look (e.g. count a requested ring's `6d × average sites` in the rule, or require the previous ring seeded): finding F1. |
| D6 | The keep-alive, explore and stranded parts of `land_season` use the model's **simplified Muster/Explore** and a `pool_owed` **fixture** (`set_account`) | Muster/Explore are W3-B's and routed tips W4-B's; the keeper's side (discovery, seeds, account lists, triggers) is what these parts test. |
| D7 | `reveal_accept_over_localnet` writes the departed-transit Holding as a fixture | Depart is W3-B's; the accept path reads only the transit record. |
| D8 | The accept path answers `409 TransitState` for a missing Holding or an empty slot | The §8.2 table lists 202/409/410/422 only; a march that does not exist is a conflict with chain state, and the relay passes the code through. |

## 5. Findings for other units and the integrator

1. **W3-A (K1):** pin the ticket score's byte form (D1) — ideally a `permutation_rules::frontier` function the program, `fclient::land::ticket_score` and the verifier (V11) all call; the keeper's `land::score_is_the_kernel_rand_over_the_raw_key` test then becomes a twin test.
2. **W3-A (K2):** SettleTicket account list as the keeper builds it (`fclient::ix::settle_ticket`): the displaced triple is present **only** for a displacement, absent for fresh/taken/expired; for `expired` the seed positions are filled but not needed (D3). If the program wants the displaced accounts optional on `taken` too, or refuses them, say so — the keeper predicts, and re-plans on a refusal.
3. **W3-A:** the keeper relies on the PS2 records JOIN, TICKET, SETTLE (outcome byte first in the payload), RELEASE, EXPLORE, TRANSIT_SETTLED (`pool_owed_delta`) and PROVINCE_OPEN with the `frontier-abi::log` key/payload widths, and on OpenRing leaving the RingSeed at status 1 with `round = ring_seed_round(now)` for `d > g`.
4. **W3-B:** the explore record's `state == 1` means pending (`frontier-abi` `explore::STATE_PENDING`), cleared by SettleExplore; DisbandStranded's refusal for a host whose Holding is present with the same gen is not named in §5.10 (the model answers `NotDormant` 46; the keeper only sends it for an absent/other-gen Holding).
5. **W4-C:** queued owner reveals are `Shared.reveals` entries `(track, json)` with the request fields plus `checked, host_id (dec string), faction, arrive, dest [P, Q], tile, region, commit_hex, close (A + W or null)`; the track state is `queued` until the pipeline moves it. `reveal_accept::check` is reusable to re-judge an entry just before sending.
6. **Contract (F1, D5):** the crowding rule can open one ring per bell while the fold has not seen the last ring's sites.
7. **Integrator:** no dependency request; `frontier-node/README.md` (not owned) could list the new land roles and the two config keys.
