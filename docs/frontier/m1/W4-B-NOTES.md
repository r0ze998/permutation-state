## 6. Findings for the integrator and the architect

- **F1 (major, architect) — a bad seal's destination is the settler's word.** For codes 1, 2 and 4 the seal does not open to the revealed plaintext, and nothing the program writes ties a transit to its destination (Reveal writes only the slot, GatherClash reads the Holding). A host whose owner revealed a bad seal (the owner knows the salt) and that **stayed** can escape the `Forfeit` if the owner settles first naming another Province; the real destination's ClashInputs then never gets its settled bit, so CloseClashInputs refuses it for ever (7.15M lamports locked) and the verifier's `BadSealSurvived` fires. Honest keepers settle at `close + 600` with the destination from the REVEAL log, so the attack is a race against a class-D keeper (P_delay 0.5). Options: (a) GatherClash takes the arrivals' Holdings writable and stamps the transit record with the destination (the delay-only path already reads them; ≤ 10 more write locks per gather); (b) SettleTransit for a bad seal requires either the revealed `(plain, salt)` (the Reveal transaction's data is public) or a proof of absence, which the program cannot check; (c) accept and rely on the keeper race plus the verifier. (a) is the smallest sound fix; it needs a layout decision for the stamp (the transit record's rsv 89..96 would hold P, Q and a flag).
- **F2 (minor) — the per-(bell, region) claim cap is per claim.** No M1 account holds a per-(bell, region) counter (DefencePool rsv 56..256 could, by amendment). Across claims the bound is the per-slot cap `defence_cap × cost` times the bell-region's slots, and per keeper the day cap.
- **F3 (major, operations) — CloseSeason cannot reach RingSeeds, AnchorArchives and DefenceClaims** (§5.2 lists them as closed by CloseSeason, but §5.7's account list has no position for them): ≈ 8.2 SOL of archive float per 7-day season, the RingSeeds' and claims' rent stay locked. Also, once the final part shrinks the Season, every instruction that reads it fails, and the final part closes the DefencePool that CloseHolding needs: the operator must close every Holding, Citizen, Province, ClashInputs, slot, day, cache before part 7 (the program can check only the funds' `provinces_opened`). Proposal: parts 8–10 with their own repeat groups (ring seeds, archives, claims) by ABI amendment, and a final-part precondition on the JoinShards' live holdings.
- **F4 (minor) — §5.11's SettleTransit list has no slot `rent_to`.** Handled by P6 (rent always returns to the Reveal's fee payer, directly or via CloseArrivalSlot).
- **F5 (keeper, W4-C)** — the program's conventions a keeper needs: ClaimDefence `day = day(now_bell)` at landing (re-plan across midnight); the SettleTransit slot index rule (P4) and the resolver/beneficiary checks (P5); ArchiveAnchors skips archived bells (a repeat is a success); CloseSeedCache `AlreadyDone` on an absent cache; EndSeason `AlreadyDone` once Ended.
- **F6 (W5-A) — thin CU margins:** ClaimDefence 24,081 / 25,000 (3.7%), CloseSeedCache 5,566 / 6,000 (7.2%). The budgets table's "measured max + 5%" rule would put them at 25.3k and 5.9k: ClaimDefence needs **26,000** if W5-A regenerates with that rule.
- **F7 (integrator, I-45) — program size.** ProgramData is now 835,629 B; the SettleTransit set after archive needs 855,637 B, under the 1-MiB working default of `reveal_loaded_limit`. W4-A's clash code will add more: account-heavy kinds should be re-checked against 1 MiB at the wave-4 merge.
- **Not W4-B's this wave** (wave note): K9 (the cohort wait in SettleTicket, `citizen.rs`) has no wave-4 owner, so it is the integrator's; the troops-return after Dissolve is W4-A's.
- **G12 items not in W4-B's files:** the ticket cohort held through `final_ts` (W3-A's SettleTicket tests). "Stranded hosts" cannot reach SettleTransit: a Holding with a transit in state 1–3 cannot be released (`HasTransits`), displaced (only provisional holdings, which cannot Depart) or re-founded before CloseHolding refunds it at the season's end.
